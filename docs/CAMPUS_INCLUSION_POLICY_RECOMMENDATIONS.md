> **Institutional Context:** Chandigarh University, Gharuan, Mohali, Punjab  
> **Date of Submission:** September 2026

---

## 📑 Executive Policy Monograph

Following empirical in-situ accessibility audits across **29 Chandigarh University academic and departmental buildings** (1,218 quantitative checkpoints evaluated, 187 physical barriers cataloged), this document presents an authoritative policy directive and capital expenditure prioritization framework for university executive leadership.

Section 45(1) of the RPWD Act, 2016 mandates that all existing public and educational buildings must be retrofitted to universal accessibility standards within five years of notification of the rules. To ensure institutional leadership and zero statutory non-compliance risk, this report outlines a phased 3-tier remediation roadmap.

---

## 🎯 Phase-Wise Strategic Remediation Framework

### Phase I: Immediate Low-Hanging Fruit & Safety Mitigations (Months 1–3)
**Primary Focus:** Eliminate immediate physical hazards, restore existing hardware, and establish digital reporting pathways.

1. **Tactile Warning Ground Surface Indicators (TGSI):**
   - Install hazard-warning tactile pavers at top and bottom landings of all central stairways across Blocks B, C, D, NC, and Zakir Husain.
   - Standard: Dot-profile rubberized or ceramic tiles ($300\times 300\text{ mm}$), positioned $300\text{ mm}$ before the first step riser.
2. **Accessible Washroom Hardware Retrofits:**
   - Invert inward-opening accessible toilet doors to outward-swinging or install sliding doors across 14 academic blocks.
   - Install heavy-duty stainless steel (AISI 304) L-shaped grab bars ($35\text{ mm}$ outer diameter) and fold-up support rails at standard $750\text{ mm}$ height.
3. **High-Contrast Step Nosing:**
   - Apply non-slip, high-contrast yellow/black abrasive tape ($50\text{ mm}$ width) to all outdoor flight stair nosings to assist students with low vision.
4. **Deploy QR Code Instant Reporting Network:**
   - Affix waterproof, high-contrast QR code plaques at main building entrances linking directly to the AccessAudit reporting portal (`/qr-report`).

---

### Phase II: Architectural Upgrades & Accessible Circulation (Months 4–8)
**Primary Focus:** Ramps, entrance thresholds, and vertical circulation retrofits.

1. **Entrance Ramp Reconstruction to Statutory Gradients:**
   - Reconstruct non-compliant entrance ramps (currently steeper than $1:10$) to the statutory **$1:12$ ($8.33\%$)** maximum slope ratio.
   - Minimum clear width: $1,200\text{ mm}$; continuous dual-height handrails at $750\text{ mm}$ and $900\text{ mm}$ with $300\text{ mm}$ horizontal extensions.
2. **Elevator Auditory & Braille Enhancements:**
   - Retrofit all campus elevators with bilingual (English & Hindi) voice floor synthesizers and tactile Braille control plates.
   - Install full-height infrared light curtains to prevent door closure on wheelchair users or crutch users.
3. **Lowered Transaction Counters:**
   - Modify university administrative reception counters, fee counters, and library circulation desks to include a lowered section ($\le 800\text{ mm}$ above finished floor) with knee clearance for wheelchair users.

---

### Phase III: Structural Capital Improvements (Months 9–18)
**Primary Focus:** Vertical access in legacy multi-story structures.

1. **Block DD Vertical Circulation Retrofit:**
   - **Critical Priority:** Academic Block DD (DD1 and DD2) currently scores **41.6%** (non-compliant) due to complete absence of elevator vertical circulation to upper floors.
   - **Recommendation:** Construct an external glass-enclosed traction elevator tower connected via covered steel gangways to floor lobbies.
2. **Inter-Block Accessible Walkway Canopy Network:**
   - Establish continuous, barrier-free, weather-protected pedestrian corridors linking Block A, Block C, Block D, and the Student Center.
   - Gradient: $\le 1:20$ without requiring intermediate landings; continuous tactile guiding tiles (directional bar profile).

## 👥 Ongoing Participatory Governance Framework

1. **Establish Campus Accessibility & Equal Opportunity Committee (CAEOC):**
   - Convene monthly reviews with student representatives with disabilities, campus estate engineering teams, and faculty auditors.
2. **Sustain AccessAudit Platform as Official Enterprise Tool:**
   - Embed AccessAudit within campus daily maintenance workflows; assign maintenance engineers to resolve tickets via the 5-stage Kanban board.
