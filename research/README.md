## 🎯 Field Research Purpose & Scope

Chandigarh University's expansive campus accommodates thousands of students across diverse academic disciplines. While modern architectural additions have prioritized contemporary design, systemic accessibility gaps have persisted for students, faculty, and visitors with disabilities (PwDs).

This ground research initiative was conducted to:
1. **Move Beyond Assumptions:** Collect empirically verified, centimetre-accurate physical dimensions of doors, ramps, corridors, elevators, and washrooms.
2. **Operationalize the RPWD Act 2016:** Enforce compliance against the statutory deadlines outlined under Section 45 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
3. **Capture Lived Experience:** Document first-hand navigation challenges faced by wheelchair users, crutch users, visually impaired students (white cane navigators), and neurodiverse learners.
4. **Deliver Actionable Engineering Solutions:** Formulate high-priority, low-cost architectural remediation plans directly consumable by university estate managers and maintenance teams.

## ⚖️ Standards Compliance Cross-Reference

Every physical dimension recorded during this audit is mapped directly against statutory benchmarks:

* **RPWD Act 2016, Section 40:** Mandatory accessibility of public buildings.
* **RPWD Act 2016, Section 42:** Accessible communication and information dissemination.
* **Harmonised Guidelines (2021) Section 4:** Ramp gradients (max 1:12), handrail heights (750mm and 900mm dual-tier), landing dimensions (1500×1500mm minimum).
* **IS 11942:2015:** Guidelines for accessible built environment and entrance facilities.
* **NBC 2016 Volume 1, Clause 4.4:** Elevator cabin dimensions (minimum 1100×1400mm clear space), audio speech synthesizers, braille buttons at 900–1200mm height.
* **WCAG 2.1 AA:** Color contrast ratios for instructional and emergency wayfinding signage (minimum 4.5:1 for standard text).
