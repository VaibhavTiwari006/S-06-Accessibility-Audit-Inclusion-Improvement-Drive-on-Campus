## 🔍 Detailed Building-by-Building Field Log

### 1. Zakir Husain Complex (Zakir A, Zakir B, Zakir C)
* **Survey Dates:** July 6, July 9, July 14, 2026  
* **Overall Score:** 57.2% | **Status:** PENDING  
* **Architectural Overview:** Historic academic wing housing lecture theatres, tutorial rooms, and faculty offices across 5 storeys.
* **Positive Findings:** Broad ground floor corridors (1800mm clear width), non-slip ceramic flooring throughout main lobbies, adequate daytime natural illumination and visual visibility across primary corridors.
* **Key Deficiencies Documented:**
  - *Main Portico Ramp:* Gradient measured steeper than the statutory 1:12 RPWD mandate. Lacks intermediate landing.
  - *Vertical Circulation:* Elevators lack audible speech synthesis announcing arrival floors. Car operating panel lacks embossed braille indicators.
  - *Sanitary Stalls:* Ground floor accessible washroom door opens inwards, blocking wheelchair turning radius.
* **Remediation Order:** Reconstruct portico ramp to 1:12 with dual handrails (750mm and 900mm). Retrofit COP with braille keys and voice synthesizer.

### 2. Nek Chand Complex (NC 1, NC 2, NC 3, NC 4, NC 5)
* **Survey Dates:** July 18, July 23, July 28, August 2, August 6, 2026  
* **Scores:** NC 1 (63.3%), NC 2 (61.2%), NC 3 (63.3%), NC 4 (63.3%), NC 5 (63.3%)  
* **Status:** NC 1, 3, 4, 5 (APPROVED) | NC 2 (PENDING)  
* **Architectural Overview:** Modern 6-storey high-density complex housing computer science laboratories, faculty cabins, and an auditorium.
* **Positive Findings:**
  - Modern dual passenger lifts accommodating wheelchairs.
  - NC 1 Auditorium equipped with accessible ground-level seating.
  - Fire exit staircases feature continuous handrails on descending flanks.
* **Key Deficiencies Documented:**
  - *Tactile Paving Deficit in NC 2:* External pedestrian walkway between parking bay and main entryway has missing hazard warning blister tiles before roadway intersections.
  - *Door Threshold Obstructions:* Several computer lab thresholds feature raised aluminium floor channels posing a tripping hazard for manual wheelchairs.
* **Remediation Order:** Complete tactile paving route to NC 2. Fit bevelled rubber transition strips across lab thresholds.

### 3. Central Academic Ring — Block D (D1 to D8)
* **Survey Dates:** August 10 to August 26, 2026  
* **Overall Score:** 74.8% | **Status:** APPROVED  
* **Architectural Overview:** Interconnected 4-storey quadrangle enclosing major engineering faculties, research centres, and seminar halls.
* **Positive Findings:**
  - Inter-block connecting skywalks at 2nd and 3rd-floor levels maintain flush, step-free circulation throughout all 8 wings.
  - Directional signage incorporates high-contrast typography.
  - Designated parking stalls located near D1 and D5 entry points.
* **Key Deficiencies Documented:**
  - *Elevator Push-Button Height:* Top floor call buttons positioned higher than the accessible reach zone.
  - *Drinking Water Stations:* High cantilevered fountains without a lowered secondary dispenser.
* **Remediation Order:** Add auxiliary low-level call buttons at 950mm. Retrofit dual-height accessible drinking water fountains in central courtyards.

---

### 4. East Campus Extension — Block DD (DD1 & DD2)
* **Survey Dates:** August 28, August 31, 2026  
* **Overall Score:** 41.6% | **Status:** REJECTED (Non-Compliant)  
* **Architectural Overview:** 3-storey extension wing containing specialized engineering workshops and departmental classrooms.
* **Positive Findings:** Ground floor workshops feature generous roll-in doors and flat floor slabs.
* **Critical Failures Documented:**
  - *Zero Vertical Mechanical Circulation:* No passenger elevator or platform lift installed. Upper floors are inaccessible to mobility-impaired students and faculty.
  - *Staircase Hazards:* Open-riser staircases without tactile warning tiles at landings or contrast nosing strips on step edges.
  - *Emergency Refuge Absence:* Upper floors lack designated refuge areas for evacuation during emergency drills.
* **Remediation Order:** Issue non-compliance notice. Recommend prioritizing vertical lift provision and temporarily scheduling classes with PwD enrollees on ground-floor levels.

### 5. Block C Cluster (C1, C2, C3)
* **Survey Dates:** September 3, September 7, September 10, 2026  
* **Scores:** C1 (79.7%), C2 (79.7%), C3 (60.3%) | **Status:** APPROVED  
* **Architectural Overview:** 5-storey academic wings housing applied sciences, biotechnology laboratories, and tiered lecture theatres.
* **Positive Findings:**
  - Tiered lecture halls in C1 and C2 feature designated level seating bays in the front and rear rows.
  - Unisex accessible restrooms present on floors 1, 3, and 5 with stainless steel grab bars.
* **Key Deficiencies Documented:**
  - C3 wing ramp entrance has surface settling causing water pooling during heavy rainfall.
  - Laboratory benches lack cut-out knee clearances for wheelchair-seated students.
* **Remediation Order:** Resurface C3 ramp with anti-skid overlay. Adjust height of designated lab benches.

### 7. Main Academic Complex — Block A (A1, A2, A3)
* **Survey Dates:** September 22, September 24, September 26, 2026  
* **Overall Score:** 96.7% | **Status:** APPROVED (Universal Design Exemplar)  
* **Architectural Overview:** Modern 6-storey facility housing the central auditorium, university administration, and high-tech seminar complexes.
* **Positive Findings:**
  - *Automated Portico Entry:* Automated sliding glass doors with safety dwell time.
  - *Smart Elevators:* Voice floor announcements in English and Hindi, high-relief tactile buttons with braille, and low-level horizontal auxiliary controls.
  - *Sanitary Standard:* Fully compliant accessible washrooms on all 6 floors with grab bars and emergency distress pull-cords.
  - *Wayfinding:* Tactile braille room identifiers positioned at standard height on latch-side of doorways.
* **Minor Observation:** Monitor glare on digital signage screens in main atrium during peak afternoon hours.
