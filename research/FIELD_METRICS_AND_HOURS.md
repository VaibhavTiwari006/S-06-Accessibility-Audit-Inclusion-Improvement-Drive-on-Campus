# 🚶 Campus Fieldwork & Physical Inspection Log
## Operational Framework & Inspection Schedule for Chandigarh University Accessibility Drive

> **Principal Field Researcher & Auditor:** Vaibhav Tiwari  
> **Campaign Period:** July – September 2026  
> **Methodology:** Systematic on-foot manual walkthroughs, physical measuring tape verification, visual assessment, and photo logging  
> **Scope:** 29 Academic & Departmental Buildings across Chandigarh University  

---

## 📋 Field Audit Framework & Phased Schedule

The campus accessibility audit was conducted in phased on-foot inspection walkthroughs across seven primary academic clusters. Each building was inspected floor by floor, evaluating accessibility along the full route of travel from the external approach to interior classrooms and facilities.

### Phase 1: Southern Zone & Nek Chand Complex (July 2026)
* **Primary Scope:** Zakir Husain Complex (Zakir A, B, C) and Nek Chand Blocks (NC 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)
* **Inspection Focus:** Main entrance porticos, external approach ramps, entrance thresholds, stair tread/riser geometry, elevator controls, and high-traffic ground-floor lecture theatres.

### Phase 2: Central Academic Ring & Workshops (August 2026)
* **Primary Scope:** Block D Wings (D1 through D8) and Block DD Extension (DD1, DD2)
* **Inspection Focus:** Departmental lab access, workshop thresholds, corridor clear widths, elevator availability, water dispenser heights, and accessible washroom stalls.

### Phase 3: Academic Blocks B & A (September 2026)
* **Primary Scope:** Block B Complex (B1 through B5) and Block A Complex (A1, A2, A3)
* **Inspection Focus:** Verification of ongoing ramp and washroom retrofits across Block B; assessment of universal design infrastructure in newer Block A facilities.

## 🔍 On-Site Inspection Workflow

For each building assessed, the auditor followed a consistent physical walkthrough sequence:

1. **Exterior Approach & Main Entryway:** Inspect kerb ramps, entry pathway surface condition, ramp slopes (via manual rise and run measurement), handrail heights, threshold height changes, and clear door opening widths.
2. **Corridors & Horizontal Circulation:** Verify minimum 1500mm clear passage width, check for wall-mounted obstructions, observe flooring condition, and review tactile guiding paths.
3. **Vertical Circulation:** Check stair step tread depth and riser height, handrail continuity, elevator car dimensions, call button heights, presence of braille markings, and voice floor announcements.
4. **Sanitary Facilities:** Measure accessible stall door width, verify outward or sliding door operation, check toilet seat height, grab bar mounting and stability, and basin clearance.
5. **Classrooms & Amenities:** Check wheelchair seating integration in lecture theatres, desktop reach clearance, and drinking water fountain accessibility.
6. **Evidentiary Documentation:** Photograph identified physical barriers, record location specifics (wing, floor, room number), and log compliance scores in the audit checklist.
